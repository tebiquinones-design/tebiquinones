About

Background

Originally from Bogotá, Colombia; based between Paris and Madrid, +7 years abroad. Founder of Alma Socialité. Forbes Under 30 in Entertainment, Spain 2025. First Latin American film to win at the inaugural Olympic Film Festival, Paris 2024.

Approach

I don't separate business from culture. The most powerful commercial moments happen when a brand stops broadcasting and starts belonging. Art, human behavior, architecture, film and entrepreneurship — not as aesthetic references, but as active strategic lenses.

I don't wait for roles to exist. I see what's missing and build it before anyone asks.
